## Set up the Mac

1. Open the zip file and move Mouse on Phone to Applications.
2. Open Mouse on Phone. Allow **Accessibility** when macOS asks.
3. Click **Pair New iPhone**. The Mac shows a QR code.

## Set up the iPhone

Get Mouse on Phone from the App Store. On the iPhone, tap **Scan Pairing Code** and scan the code on your Mac.

## What it does

- A full-screen trackpad with Mac gestures and Force Touch.
- Keyboard, media keys, custom panels, apps and menus.
- Live View and a second display for your Mac.
- Control an iPad over Bluetooth: the iPhone becomes its keyboard and mouse.

## Privacy

Mouse on Phone works only between your own devices. It has no account and no analytics. Read the [privacy policy](privacy/) and the [support page](support/).
