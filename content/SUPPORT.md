# Mouse on Phone support

The same fixes are in the app under Help.

**Contact:** [open an issue on GitHub](@ISSUES_URL@). Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Mac model and
macOS version. If the app crashed, you can attach crash reports: on the iPhone, open
Settings in Mouse on Phone > Help > Share Crash Reports.

There are two apps: **Mouse on Phone** on the iPhone or iPad (from the App Store), and
**Mouse on Phone** on the Mac (a free download).

## Getting started

1. [Download Mouse on Phone for your Mac](@DOWNLOAD_URL@) and open the zip file.
2. Allow **Accessibility** when the Mac app asks (System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   Accessibility). For Live View and the second display, also allow **Screen Recording**.
3. In the Mac app, click **Pair New iPhone**.
4. On your iPhone, open Mouse on Phone, tap **Scan Pairing Code**, and scan the code on the Mac.

## Common questions

**The Mac does not appear on my iPhone.**
Make sure Mouse on Phone is open on the Mac and both devices use the same Wi-Fi. On the
iPhone, check Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network > Mouse on Phone.

**The pointer does not move.**
On the Mac, turn on Mouse on Phone in System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Accessibility. If it is already on, turn it off and on again. If the Mac app shows
**Restart Mouse on Phone**, click it.

**Live View or the second display is black.**
On the Mac, turn on Mouse on Phone in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen &
System Audio Recording, then reopen it.

**Scripts, Shortcuts or shut down do nothing.**
These are off for safety. Turn them on in the Mouse on Phone window on the Mac. When
macOS asks to let Mouse on Phone control System Events, click OK.

**How do I remove an iPhone?**
In the Mouse on Phone window on the Mac, click **Revoke** next to the iPhone. To pair it
again, click Pair New iPhone and scan the new code.

**Is my connection secure?**
Yes. Only iPhones you pair by scanning the code on your Mac can connect, and all traffic is
encrypted. Nothing is sent off your local network.

**Can I control my iPad?**
Yes. On the iPhone, open Mouse on Phone and tap Control an iPad over Bluetooth. On the iPad, open
Mouse on Phone, tap Use an iPhone as the Trackpad, and tap the iPhone. iPadOS does not list the iPhone
in its Bluetooth settings. Pointer, clicks, scrolling, typing and media keys work. Multi-finger
iPad gestures are available only with Apple trackpads.

**Can I use more than one device?**
Yes. Several iPhones and iPads can control the same Mac at once. On the iPhone, tap the Devices
button next to Settings on the trackpad to switch between your paired Macs and connected iPads.
With more than one iPad connected, input goes only to the iPad you choose.
