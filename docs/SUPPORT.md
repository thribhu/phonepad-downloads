# PhonePad support

The same fixes are in the app under Help.

**Contact:** [open an issue on GitHub](@ISSUES_URL@). Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Mac model and
macOS version. If PhonePad crashed, you can attach crash reports: on the iPhone, open
Settings in PhonePad > Help > Share Crash Reports.

## Getting started

1. [Download PhonePad for Mac](@DOWNLOAD_URL@) and open the zip file.
2. Allow **Accessibility** when the Mac app asks (System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   Accessibility). For Live View and the second display, also allow **Screen Recording**.
3. In the Mac app, click **Pair New iPhone**.
4. On your iPhone, open PhonePad, tap **Scan Pairing Code**, and scan the code on the Mac.

## Common questions

**The Mac does not appear on my iPhone.**
Make sure PhonePad is open on the Mac and both devices use the same Wi-Fi. On the iPhone,
check Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network > PhonePad.

**The pointer does not move.**
On the Mac, turn on PhonePad in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
If it is already on, turn it off and on again.

**Live View or the second display is black.**
On the Mac, turn on PhonePad in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio
Recording, then reopen PhonePad.

**Scripts, Shortcuts or shut down do nothing.**
These are off for safety. Turn them on in the PhonePad window on the Mac. When macOS asks to
let PhonePad control System Events, click OK.

**How do I remove an iPhone?**
In the PhonePad window on the Mac, click **Revoke** next to the iPhone. To pair it again,
click Pair New iPhone and scan the new code.

**Is my connection secure?**
Yes. Only iPhones you pair by scanning the code on your Mac can connect, and all traffic is
encrypted. PhonePad sends nothing off your local network.

**Can I control my iPad?**
Yes, as a Labs feature (Beta). On the iPhone, turn on Labs at the bottom of the start screen,
then tap Control an iPad over Bluetooth. On the iPad, open PhonePad, tap Use an iPhone as the
Trackpad, and tap the iPhone. iPadOS does not list the iPhone in its Bluetooth settings.
Pointer, clicks, scrolling, typing and media keys work. Multi-finger iPad gestures are
available only with Apple trackpads.

**Can I control my Apple TV?**
Yes, as a Labs feature (Beta), with nothing installed on the Apple TV. Turn on Labs at the
bottom of PhonePad's start screen. Connect the iPhone and the Apple TV to the same Wi-Fi, tap
the Apple TV on the start screen, and type the PIN that the TV shows. Swipe
to move, tap to select, and use Controls > Remote for the buttons. To remove PhonePad from the
Apple TV, open Settings > Remotes and Devices on the TV.
